2. What the app is for

SnoreTrace records sound while you sleep and presents it afterwards as a volume timeline with the snoring events it found. It is a recording and visualisation tool for your own reference, and nothing more. Detection is not perfect: everyday sounds may be marked as snoring, and quiet snoring may be missed. Please read the figures the app shows as a rough record of the night rather than as a measurement.

3. Recording in a shared room

You are responsible for how you record in your own environment. The microphone captures whatever is audible in the room, so anyone sharing it with you may be recorded. Please tell them beforehand and obtain their agreement where the law of your region requires it. The developer has no access to your recordings and cannot act on them in any way.
